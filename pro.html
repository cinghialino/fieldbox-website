Fieldbox Pro

The peace of mind you deserve
after capturing the incredible.

After the job, you can rest easy knowing what was copied, compared, confirmed, and handed off. Fieldbox Pro gives you that feeling of having a proper backup.

Download Fieldbox Backup, then choose monthly, yearly, or Lifetime Pro in the app.

What Pro helps you finish

The whole production stays accounted for.

Pro adds the capacity to carry a multi-card job from the first ingest to the final client handoff without losing sight of what the app has recorded.

Multi-card sessions

Keep every card in the same session.

Plan the job ahead of time, then add cards across a multi-day shoot while the session keeps its name, files, and recorded history together.

Multiple destinations

Send each session where it belongs.

Connect more than one supported cloud or NAS destination, choose a destination set for each session, and see each provider's confirmation separately.

Working copies

Make selects before you get home.

Create separate working copies through the share sheet or Save to Photos without changing the verified archive.

Client handoff

Hand off verified material immediately.

Copy a verified session to an attached drive and include optional integrity material for the client or collaborator.

The work stays visible

See what you are carrying home.

From a multi-day ingest to a client handoff, the current state remains readable on the iPhone or iPad you already carry.

Share selected working copies on the spot
Manage multiple cloud and NAS connections
Review every recorded session event
Hand a verified session to a client on an attached drive
