Fieldbox Pro

More destinations.
The same verified core.

Pro adds multiple cloud and NAS connections, concurrent destination uploads, team accounts, and client delivery. The underlying card transfer and source-to-local SHA-256 comparison remain the same.

Get notified at launch Pro ships alongside the app. Email gets you a heads-up the day it does.
Destinations
Multiple Cloud Connections
Connect more than one supported cloud or NAS destination on the same device.
Uploads
Concurrent Destination Sync
After a transfer, Fieldbox can upload the session to every enabled destination concurrently. Each destination keeps its own recorded confirmation state.
Crews
Team Accounts
Use one shared cloud login while sessions from each device stay in their own namespace. Fieldbox applies ownership boundaries when Cloud Browser opens a session.
Handoff
Client Delivery
Export a session to a USB-C drive and include its verification material for a client or collaborator to review.
