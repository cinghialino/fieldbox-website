Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

Overview

Fieldbox Backup ("the app", "we", "us") is a professional photo backup application for iOS developed by ZANZAN, LLC. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and your rights.

Your media, filenames, paths, hashes, session details, and cloud credentials stay on your device and in storage accounts you choose. If you explicitly enable Anonymous Usage Data, the app sends a small identifier-free weekly summary to a Fieldbox telemetry receiver as described below.

Data the App Accesses

Fieldbox Backup accesses only the following data, solely to perform the backup functions you initiate:

  • Camera card contents — image and video files you choose to back up. Read-only. The app never modifies or deletes files from your card.
  • Device storage — verified local files are written to Fieldbox's private app-managed archive. The archive is not editable in Files. An export creates a separate copy at a destination you select.
  • Your cloud storage accounts — when you configure a cloud destination, the app uploads your backed-up files to that account. See the sections below for each provider.

Google Drive

When you connect Google Drive, Fieldbox Backup requests limited, app-scoped access. This grants the app access only to files and folders that Fieldbox Backup itself creates — it cannot read any other files in your Google Drive.

Specifically, the app:

  • Creates a FieldBox folder in your Google Drive
  • Uploads your backed-up photo and video files into that folder
  • Checks uploaded files using the strongest verification information each provider exposes: stored size for all supported providers and a matching SHA-256 checksum where the provider supplies one
  • Can remove cloud files only through the app's deliberate Cloud Browser deletion action; turning off session backup pauses future uploads and keeps existing copies

Fieldbox Backup does not read, modify, or delete any file in your Google Drive that it did not create. Your Google account credentials are stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device and are never transmitted to us.

Dropbox

When you connect Dropbox, Fieldbox Backup uses App Folder access — an isolated folder named Fieldbox Backup inside your Dropbox Apps/ directory. The app can only read and write within that folder. It cannot access any other part of your Dropbox.

Microsoft OneDrive

When you connect OneDrive, Fieldbox Backup requests limited, app-scoped access. This grants the app access only to its own dedicated app folder in your OneDrive — it cannot read, modify, or delete any other file in your account.

Your Microsoft account credentials are stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device and are never transmitted to us.

Backblaze B2 / S3-Compatible Storage

When you configure an S3-compatible destination (Backblaze B2, Wasabi, Cloudflare R2, etc.), you provide your own access credentials. The app uses those credentials solely to upload, verify, and delete files in the bucket you specify. Credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain and never leave your device.

NAS (WebDAV)

When you configure your own NAS or WebDAV server as a destination, you provide the server address and your own credentials. The app uses those credentials solely to upload, verify, and delete files in the folder you specify on your server. Connections require HTTPS. Credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain and are sent only to the server you configured.

Optional Anonymous Usage Data

Anonymous Usage Data is off by default. The first-run walkthrough offers an unchecked choice, and you can inspect or change it later in Settings. When enabled, Fieldbox combines approved counters on your device and sends one summary only after a calendar week ends.

A summary can contain the schema version, calendar week, app version, iOS major version, device class, coarse file-count and size ranges, and counters for completed or failed scans, imports, restores, cloud-provider operations, and S3-compatible multipart uploads.

The summary does not include a persistent device or user identifier, media, filenames, paths, hashes, card or session identity, camera details, account information, credentials, exact file counts, exact sizes, exact times, locations, raw errors, advertising identifiers, or contact information. Because there is no stable identifier, summaries are not designed to follow one device or person across weeks.

Summaries are sent to telemetry.fieldbox.app, a Fieldbox-controlled Cloudflare Worker. The receiver validates a fixed allowlist and stores only approved summary fields in Cloudflare Workers Analytics Engine. The Worker is configured not to read or store IP addresses or other request metadata, and Worker observability is disabled. Cloudflare necessarily processes network requests to provide and secure its service under its own Privacy Policy.

Turning sharing off immediately deletes unsent summaries from the device. Accepted summaries are retained by Workers Analytics Engine for its fixed three-month retention period and then expire.

Apple Private Key-Value Storage

Fieldbox may use Apple's private iCloud key-value service to keep only the number of free verified imports used across devices signed in to the same Apple Account. It does not place media, archives, sessions, credentials, or cloud backups in iCloud, and iCloud is not a Fieldbox backup destination or restore source.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No automatic crash reports sent to Fieldbox
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No names, email addresses, precise locations, or Fieldbox user accounts
  • No reading of your Photos library; user-initiated Save to Photos uses add-only access
  • No sale of personal data and no data used for advertising or tracking

Data Retention

Local archive files remain in Fieldbox's app-managed container until you delete them through the app or remove the app and its data. Exported copies remain at the destinations you selected. Cloud copies remain in your cloud account until you remove them through Fieldbox or through your provider. Optional anonymous summaries follow the retention described above.

Security

Cloud credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain using device-only protection and are excluded from device backups. All cloud and telemetry communication uses HTTPS/TLS. Local file integrity is checked with SHA-256; cloud verification is provider-specific as described above.

Children's Privacy

Fieldbox Backup is a professional tool intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.
