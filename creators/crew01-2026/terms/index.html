Participation terms

Fieldbox Crew01

These terms apply only to the invitation-only Crew01 creator campaign. They do not apply to future Fieldbox campaigns.

1. Enrollment and complimentary access

Eligible invited creators may receive one complimentary month of Fieldbox Pro. Enrollment does not require a post, positive review, or endorsement. Redemption details are provided individually after enrollment and may not be transferred or resold.

2. Publishing window and rewards

The publishing window is the creator's complimentary month. One qualifying video released during that month earns a one-year Fieldbox license. Three or more qualifying videos released during that same month earn a lifetime Fieldbox license. The lifetime reward replaces, rather than adds to, the one-year reward.

3. Qualifying videos

Each video must be a publicly accessible Instagram Reel or TikTok, run for at least 20 seconds, feature Fieldbox as a meaningful part of the content, be published during the creator's complimentary month, and include #FieldboxBackup and #FieldboxCrew01 in the published caption. One public URL must be submitted for each video.

4. Honest experience and disclosure

Creators must describe only their actual experience and may be critical. Positive sentiment is not required. Each video must clearly disclose within the video that Fieldbox provided complimentary access. The creator is responsible for following applicable laws and platform rules for endorsements and sponsored or gifted access.

5. Review

Fieldbox reviews submitted links for the published date, platform, duration, required hashtags, public availability, meaningful product use, and disclosure. Spam, copied or fraudulent submissions, inaccessible links, and content that does not meet the stated requirements do not qualify. Fieldbox may request clarification before making a determination.

6. Content ownership and sharing

Creators keep ownership of their content. Submitting a public link allows Fieldbox to link to or embed the public post for campaign administration and reporting. Fieldbox will request separate permission before downloading or reposting the creator's video as a new post on a Fieldbox-owned channel.

7. Product accuracy and privacy

Creators must not expose client information, account credentials, access keys, or other private information. Creators must not make guarantees about permanent safety or recovery, claim that Fieldbox repairs damaged cards, or present an unreleased feature as currently available.

8. Campaign records and closure

Enrollment details and submitted links are handled as described in the Fieldbox Privacy Policy. Crew01 may be closed to new responses, but its URLs will not be reused for another campaign. Closing the campaign does not remove a reward already earned under these terms.

9. Contact

Questions, corrections, or withdrawal requests can be sent to creators@fieldbox.app.
