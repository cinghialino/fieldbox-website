Getting started

What does Fieldbox Backup actually do?

It takes the files off a memory card, copies them onto your iPhone or iPad, checks every single one to make sure the copy is perfect, and then sends a copy to a cloud storage account you already own. By the time it's done, your files exist in three places: the card, your device, and the cloud.

It's built with professional shoots in mind, but it works just as well for a drone flight, an interview or podcast recorded on a separate audio recorder, or a card full of vacation photos. Anything that comes off a memory card gets the same verified treatment.

How do I back up a card?

Plug your card reader into your phone or iPad, open Fieldbox, and choose the card. Give the shoot a name (or add it to one you already started), glance over the summary of what's on the card, and tap Start. When the screen turns green and says every file is verified, you're done.

Example

You finish a session and swap cards mid-shoot. Fieldbox recognizes the camera and offers to drop the new card straight into the same session, so everything from the day ends up together.

What is a "session," exactly?

A session is just the shoot or the day you're working on, one place that holds everything from it. Give it a name when you start (the client, the event, the trip) and every card you import that day gets added to the same one.

Different cameras can feed the same session too. Fieldbox keeps each camera's files sorted into their own spot underneath it automatically, so nothing from two bodies ever gets mixed together, even while it all lives under one name.

Example

A full day covering a product launch: morning interviews on one camera, afternoon b-roll on a second body, and a drone card at the end. All three imports land in one session called "product-launch," so the whole day is in one place when you look back.

Do I need an internet connection to back up?

No. Copying files off the card and checking them happens entirely on your device, so it works in a basement, a tent, or a plane. Once you're back online, Fieldbox picks up any backups that are waiting and sends them to your cloud on its own, even if it's not the same day.

Can I plan a session before the shoot even starts? Pro

Yes. Set up a session with a name and a date ahead of time, and pick which cloud it should go to. On the day, it sits at the top of the list ready to go, so you can plug in and start without stopping to name anything.

Verification and source media

What does "verified" actually mean?

Every file gets a unique fingerprint before it's copied, and another one after. If the two don't match exactly, down to a single byte, Fieldbox knows and retries the file rather than letting a broken copy through quietly. Only once every file on the card has matched perfectly does the screen turn green.

Is my card ever erased or changed?

Never automatically. Fieldbox only reads from your card; it does not format, delete from, or reorganize it. The card remains the original source until you decide to reuse it. The app records when every file matched at transfer time and never formats anything on its own.

What happens if a transfer gets interrupted?

Whatever file was copying at that moment finishes safely or isn't counted at all, there's no half-written file left behind. Fieldbox tells you exactly how many files made it in and shows a standing warning not to format the card until the rest are backed up. Plug the card back in later and it continues from where it stopped, it never starts over.

If I stop subscribing to Pro, do I lose my backups?

No, never. Everything you already backed up stays fully accessible: your archive, browsing, previews, and every verification record. A lapsed subscription only pauses the Pro conveniences, like uploading to several clouds at once. Nothing you own is ever locked.

Cloud backup

Which cloud services can I use?

Dropbox, Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, any S3-compatible storage (Backblaze B2, Wasabi, Cloudflare R2, and similar), or your own NAS and home server over WebDAV (Synology, QNAP, TrueNAS, Nextcloud, and others). It's your account and your storage, Fieldbox just moves the verified files there.

The standard app connects one of these at a time. Pro adds the rest, so you can connect several and send a backup to all of them at once.

How does Fieldbox know a cloud backup really landed, not just that the upload didn't error?

Fieldbox always SHA-256 verifies the local archive against the card. Cloud status is a separate provider receipt: S3-compatible storage and Google Drive require a matching provider-reported SHA-256 checksum; Dropbox and WebDAV confirm stored size; OneDrive uses its checksum when available and otherwise confirms stored size. The app labels upload confirmation separately from cloud hash verification.

Can I restore files that are missing from my device?

Yes. From a session, choose Restore Locally Missing Files and select the original card or a cloud provider that has confirmed the complete session. A cloud download first goes to staging. Fieldbox returns it to the archive only when its SHA-256 matches the hash recorded from the original transfer.

I backed up to Dropbox, then switched to Google Drive for the next shoot. Did my old backup stop counting?

No. Once a shoot has a complete cloud-confirmed copy, that record stays with that provider. Switching your default only decides where new backups go from now on; it never touches an existing cloud copy.

Can I back up to more than one cloud at the same time? Pro

Yes. Pick as many clouds as you want for a session and they all upload together, not one after another. The success screen tells you how many confirmed.

Does Fieldbox use my iCloud storage?

No, it never touches iCloud. Your files go to your device and to whichever cloud account you connect yourself. There's no subscription or storage tied to Fieldbox itself, you use the storage you already have.

Working as a team

Can my whole crew back up to one shared cloud account? Pro

Yes, hand everyone the same login. Every device that connects gets its own space inside the account, so nobody's files ever collide or overwrite each other.

Example

A three-person crew covers a conference: two shooting photo and video, one recording the keynote audio on a separate device, all backing up into the same studio Dropbox. A producer can browse every device's files from any phone, but nobody can delete what someone else uploaded. Nobody can wipe out a colleague's only copy by accident.

Devices & files

What do I need to use Fieldbox?
  • An iPhone 15 Pro / 16 or later, or an iPad with USB-C, running iOS or iPadOS 17 or newer
  • A USB-C card reader for whatever your camera, drone, or recorder uses, SD, microSD, CompactFlash, CFast, XQD, CFexpress, and most others. If it shows up as a drive on your device, Fieldbox can read it.

That's the whole list, no extra hardware, no dedicated device to carry. You don't need an internet connection to back up either, that part happens entirely on your device. You'll just want one eventually so your cloud copy can go through.

What file types does it handle?

Just about anything a memory card can hold:

  • RAW from every major camera brand (Canon, Nikon, Sony, Fujifilm, Panasonic, Olympus, Pentax, and Adobe's universal DNG)
  • Regular stills: JPEG, HEIC, PNG, TIFF
  • Video, including the higher-end formats used by cinema cameras and drones
  • Audio, from a quick voice memo to a full field recorder's output

If it came off a camera, a drone, or an audio recorder, Fieldbox almost certainly recognizes it.

Can I preview my photos and videos in the app?

Yes. Browse a backed-up session as a thumbnail grid and tap anything to view it full-size, RAW and HEIC included.

Can I hear the audio, like sound in a video or a separate recording?

Yes. Playing back a video plays its audio right along with it, so you can review an interview or a narration take without leaving the app. A standalone audio file, say a take from a handheld recorder, opens with its own playback controls too.

Can I copy a backup onto a USB drive to hand off to a client? Pro

Yes, export a session directly to any USB-C drive you already own, formatted exFAT, FAT32, or APFS, no need to buy anything new for it. Handy for a same-day client handoff without needing a laptop.
