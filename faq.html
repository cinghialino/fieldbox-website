Getting started

What does Fieldbox Backup actually do?

It copies files from a memory card to your iPhone or iPad and compares source and local SHA-256 values for each selected file. You can then send the verified local archive to a cloud storage account you already own. Backup Status reports the current copy inventory and the confirmations Fieldbox has recorded.

It is built for photographers, videographers, drone operators, sound engineers, and mixed-media crews using cameras, drones, and supported audio recorders. The supported-format list below shows the extensions Fieldbox currently recognizes.

How do I back up a card?

Plug your card reader into your phone or iPad, open Fieldbox, and choose the card. Give the shoot a name or add it to an existing session, then review every scanned file as selected, already verified, or deliberately excluded. Choose a Photography, Hybrid Shoot, or Video preset as a starting point, customize it if needed, and tap Start. When the transfer completes, review the recorded outcomes before deciding what to do with the card.

Example

You finish a session and swap cards mid-shoot. Fieldbox recognizes the camera and offers to drop the new card straight into the same session, so everything from the day ends up together.

What is a "session," exactly?

A session is just the shoot or the day you're working on, one place that holds everything from it. Give it a name when you start (the client, the event, the trip) and every card you import that day gets added to the same one.

Different cameras can feed the same session too. Fieldbox keeps each camera's files sorted into their own spot underneath it automatically, so nothing from two bodies ever gets mixed together, even while it all lives under one name.

Example

A full day covering a product launch: morning interviews on one camera, afternoon b-roll on a second body, and a drone card at the end. All three imports land in one session called "product-launch," so the whole day is in one place when you look back.

Do I need an internet connection to back up?

No. Copying files off the card and checking them happens entirely on your device, so it works in a basement, a tent, or a plane. When Fieldbox is active again with a connection available, it can resume pending uploads to the enabled destination.

Can I plan a session before the shoot even starts?

Yes, with Pro. Set up a session with a name, date, and destination choices ahead of time. Once a transfer begins, it becomes a regular session and remains in the archive. If no transfer starts, the plan expires 30 days after its planned date.

Verification and source media

What does "verified" actually mean?

Fieldbox calculates a SHA-256 value from each selected source file and from the local copy. If the values do not match, the app retries the file and flags it if it still cannot verify a match. The green result appears only when the selected files have matched their local copies.

Is my card ever erased or changed?

Never automatically. Fieldbox only reads from your card; it does not format, delete from, or reorganize it. The card remains the original source until you decide to reuse it. The app records when every file matched at transfer time and never formats anything on its own.

What happens if a transfer gets interrupted?

A file is promoted into the archive only after its local verification succeeds. After an interruption, Fieldbox reports the files it recorded as verified and keeps the transfer plan and per-file checkpoints. It resumes only after the reconnected source matches the recorded card identity, selected folder, inventory, and checkpoint evidence. You can also discard the interrupted transfer and its partial local files.

If I stop subscribing to Pro, do I lose my backups?

No. Fieldbox does not delete the on-device archive or existing cloud copies when a subscription ends. Every session remains readable. You can choose up to three active sessions that may continue receiving files and change that selection later. Other Pro actions, including multiple-destination uploads, working-copy exports, archive repair, local-file management, and USB Transfer, pause until Pro is active again.

Cloud backup

Which cloud services can I use?

Dropbox, Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, any S3-compatible storage (Backblaze B2, Wasabi, Cloudflare R2, and similar), or your own NAS and home server over WebDAV (Synology, QNAP, TrueNAS, Nextcloud, and others). It's your account and your storage, Fieldbox just moves the verified files there.

FieldBox Core connects one of these at a time. Pro adds multiple connections, so you can send a session to several enabled destinations concurrently.

How does Fieldbox know a cloud backup really landed, not just that the upload didn't error?

Fieldbox always compares source and local SHA-256 values. Cloud status is separate provider evidence: the app records a matching provider-reported checksum where one is available. Otherwise it records provider acceptance or object existence together with the expected stored size. Upload confirmation remains distinct from cloud hash verification.

Read more about cloud verification →
Can I restore files that are missing from my device?

Yes. From a session, choose Restore Locally Missing Files and select the original card or a cloud provider that has confirmed the complete session. A cloud download first goes to staging. Fieldbox returns it to the archive only when its SHA-256 matches the hash recorded from the original transfer.

I backed up to Dropbox, then switched to Google Drive for the next shoot. Did my old backup stop counting?

No. Once a shoot has a complete cloud-confirmed copy, that record stays with that provider. Switching your default only decides where new backups go from now on; it never touches an existing cloud copy.

Can I back up to more than one cloud at the same time? Pro

Yes. Choose the enabled destinations for a session and Fieldbox uploads to them concurrently. Each destination keeps its own recorded confirmation state.

Does Fieldbox use my iCloud storage?

Fieldbox never uses iCloud as a media-backup destination or restore source. Your files go to the app-managed archive and to whichever supported storage account you connect. Apple's private services may sync the number of free verified imports used across your devices and, only when you deliberately request it, hold a settings backup. That settings backup contains app preferences, camera profiles, and memory-card details. It never contains media, archives, sessions, cloud connections, or provider credentials.

Working as a team

Can my whole crew back up to one shared cloud account?

Yes. Each connected device uses its own space within the shared account, so Fieldbox keeps one device’s session paths separate from another’s. Cloud Browser also applies ownership boundaries when opened from a session.

Example

A three-person crew covers a conference: two shooting photo and video, one recording the keynote audio on a separate device, all backing up into the same studio Dropbox. A producer can browse the cloud inventory, while session-launched Cloud Browser applies the app’s ownership boundaries to deletion controls.

Devices & files

What do I need to use Fieldbox?
  • An iPhone or iPad with USB-C, running iOS or iPadOS 17 or newer
  • A USB-C card reader for whatever your camera, drone, or recorder uses, SD, microSD, CompactFlash, CFast, XQD, CFexpress, and most others. If it shows up as a drive on your device, Fieldbox can read it.

That's the whole list, no extra hardware, no dedicated device to carry. You don't need an internet connection to back up either, that part happens entirely on your device. You'll just want one eventually so your cloud copy can go through.

What file types does it handle?

Fieldbox currently recognizes these extensions. Matching is case-insensitive:

  • RAW: 3FR, CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF, RW2, ORF, DNG, PEF, SRW
  • Stills: JPG, JPEG, HEIC, HEIF, PNG, TIF, TIFF
  • Video: MOV, MP4, M4V, AVI, MXF, BRAW, R3D
  • Audio: WAV, AIF, AIFF, MP3, FLAC, M4A, CAF, BWF, RF64
  • Sidecars and metadata: XMP, XML, JSON

Every recognized file follows the same copy and source/local SHA-256 comparison workflow. Preview availability is separate and depends on the formats supported by iOS.

Can I preview my photos and videos in the app?

Browse a backed-up session as a thumbnail grid and tap a file to open it. Preview and playback depend on iOS support for that specific format. A recognized file can still be copied and compared even when iOS cannot render a preview.

Can I hear the audio, like sound in a video or a separate recording?

Yes. Playing back a video plays its audio right along with it, so you can review an interview or a narration take without leaving the app. A standalone audio file, say a take from a handheld recorder, opens with its own playback controls too.

Can I copy a backup onto a USB drive to hand off to a client? Pro

Yes. Fieldbox Pro's USB Transfer copies one verified session or a selection of sessions to an attached drive formatted as exFAT, FAT32, or APFS. You can optionally include integrity sidecars so the recipient can compare each delivered file's SHA-256 value offline.
