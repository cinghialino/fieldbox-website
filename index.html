A Note From the Photographer Who Built This

I'm a photographer. I know the ritual: cards fill up mid-shoot, and at some point you have to trust that a copy actually worked before you reuse them. A progress bar finishing isn't the same as knowing every file landed intact — and on a job that can't be reshot, that gap is the whole problem.

Fieldbox Backup exists because I wanted an honest answer to one question: is this file really, verifiably safe, in more than one place, before I touch the format button? Not "it looks done." Verified — a SHA-256 checksum on the original and on the copy, matched, with a clear green screen when it's true and an unambiguous red one when it isn't.

It's built and maintained by one person — me. That means it moves slower than a team-built app, but every decision in it is made by someone who actually needs it to work in a hotel room at midnight with a card full of a day you can't get back.

— Alessandro Casagli
