Field Backup System

Back up your cards.
Verify every file.
Know before you format.

Fieldbox Backup copies your memory card onto your phone, checks every single file to make sure nothing's missing or broken, then uploads a verified copy to your own cloud storage once you're connected. You'll know every file is verified before you ever reuse the card.

Get notified at launch Not on the App Store yet — email gets you a heads-up the day it ships.
Integrity
Every File Checked
Every file is checked twice, once on the card and once after copying (SHA-256). Bit-perfect, or it's flagged.
Cloud
Your Cloud, Your Choice
Dropbox, Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, or your own S3-compatible bucket (Backblaze B2, Wasabi, R2) — your choice, your account.
Offline
Works Without Wi-Fi
Local transfer and verification work fully offline, so you can start right away. Connect to Wi-Fi as soon as you can. Your backup isn't complete under the 3-2-1 rule until cloud sync finishes.
Session Log
Every Transfer Logged
Every transfer logged with file counts, sizes, checksums, and cloud confirmation.
Formats
Every File Type
RAW, JPEG, HEIC, video, and audio, from cameras, drones, and recorders alike.
Organization
Sessions, Not Chaos
Every card you import groups into one named session, even across multiple cameras.

A Note From the Photographer Who Built This

I'm a photographer. Fieldbox Backup exists to answer one question: has this file actually been verified, on the card and on my device, byte for byte. A SHA-256 checksum on the original and the copy, matched, green when true, red when it isn't.

I build and maintain it myself, so every decision comes from someone who actually uses it on real shoots.

- Alessandro Casagli
