Integrity
Compare Before You Move On
Fieldbox compares the SHA-256 value read from the card with the copied local file. A mismatch remains visible instead of being marked verified.
Cloud

Local transfer and verification do not require a connection. Cloud status is recorded separately when a provider reports a complete copy.

Why I Built Fieldbox

I built Fieldbox for the parts of professional work that happen away from a desk. On assignment, I may need an offsite copy before leaving a location. When I travel with a camera or drone, I can generate more photographs and footage than the memory cards I have with me can hold. And sometimes I need to send work to the cloud or make it available to a client while I am still on the move.

In each case, I already have most of what I need in my bag or pocket: an iPhone or iPad, a card reader, and a connection whenever one becomes available. What I wanted was a straightforward way to turn those pieces into a disciplined backup workflow, with a clear record of what has been copied and verified.

That is why I made Fieldbox. It copies photographs, video, audio, and other files from a memory card to your device, compares each copied file with the source using SHA-256, and can replicate it to your own cloud storage. Together, those copies can form a practical 3-2-1 workflow built around hardware you already carry.

For me, the value is confidence based on clear evidence, not guesswork. I can see when a transfer has been verified and make the next decision with the facts in front of me.

I continue to build and maintain Fieldbox from the perspective of someone who uses cameras professionally. Every decision is shaped by the realities of working on set, traveling with limited equipment, and coming home with files that matter.

Alessandro Casagli
