Verified backup for iPhone and iPad

Back up cards
without a laptop.

Fieldbox copies card-based photos, video, and audio to your iPhone or iPad, compares source and local SHA-256 values, and can replicate the complete session to storage you choose. Built for photographers, videographers, drone operators, sound engineers, and other crews working away from a laptop.

A clear workflow from card to cloud.

Fieldbox keeps the transfer, local comparison, and provider confirmation visible as separate stages, so the current state of each session remains easy to read.

Transfer Review
Every Scanned File Gets an Outcome
Review what will transfer, what was already verified in the session, and what you deliberately excluded. Photography, Hybrid Shoot, and Video presets are editable starting points.
Integrity
Compare Before You Move On
Fieldbox compares the SHA-256 value read from the card with the copied local file. A mismatch remains visible instead of being marked verified.
Cloud
Use Storage You Control
Connect Dropbox, Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, an S3-compatible bucket such as Backblaze B2, Wasabi, or R2, or your own WebDAV/NAS storage.
Offline
Transfer First, Sync When Ready
Local transfer and verification do not require a connection. Cloud status is recorded separately when a provider reports a complete copy.
Session Log
Read the Session, Not a Guess
Each session provides useful details about the ingested files, including their counts and sizes, source-to-local SHA-256 comparison results, and the confirmation status reported by each connected cloud provider.
Formats
Made for Card Media
Import recognized RAW photographs, still images, video, audio, and sidecars. Support includes Hasselblad 3FR, Canon CR2/CR3, Nikon NEF, Sony ARW, Fujifilm RAF, DNG, BRAW, R3D, and other listed formats.
Organization
Keep a Shoot Together
Group cards from the same job into one named session, including cards from more than one camera.
Integrity Record
Export the Evidence
Create a signed record of a session’s verification evidence and share the resulting .fieldbox.json file. It is self-issued by the device, not externally certified.

Why I Built Fieldbox

Fieldbox started with a practical need: back up camera cards while working away from a laptop. I already carried an iPhone or iPad and a card reader, but I wanted a clear record of what had been copied and verified.

Fieldbox copies card media into its app-managed archive, compares source and local SHA-256 values, and can replicate the complete session to storage you choose. It is the tool I wanted in the field: direct about what it measured and built around equipment I already carry.

Alessandro Casagli
